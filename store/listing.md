# Chrome Web Store listing — SCATERNET

## Name
SCATERNET — the internet, on ska

## Summary (max 132 chars)
Flip it on and every page turns into a ska/scat catastrophe: scat gibberish, jazz-ska cartoons, brown stains and terrible skanking music.

## Category
Fun / Entertainment

## Language
English

## Detailed description
SCATERNET is a joke. Flip it on and the entire web turns into an unlistenable,
unreadable ska catastrophe:

🎷 Every word becomes scat gibberish — "skabidibi doubidou bada boudi booo da bi
    doo bi do baaaba" — in wildly varied sizes, fonts and RANDOM CAPS, sprinkled
    with jazz emoji.
🎸 Every image becomes a jazz-ska musician — ultra-realistic photos of
    dreadlocked, pork-pie-hatted sax players, trumpeters and bassists.
🥁 Every page plays music — a bed of ska, layered scat vocals and honking sax
    solos, all looping over each other.
💨 Every mouse click makes a noise — a scat syllable, or one of dozens of farts.
🎬 Every video gets a ska track layered on top of its own sound (yes, on top).
🟤 Brown stains, scattered all over the page, for good measure.

Click the note to turn it on. Click it again to turn it off — the page snaps
right back to boring old human internet. There's a volume slider and a "mute all
sound" switch in the popup for when you need to be able to hear yourself think.

Great for: pranking a friend's laptop, or finding out what the web sounds like
after three too many.

No account. No sign-up. No data leaves your browser. Everything — every image,
every sound — is baked into the extension.

## Single purpose (required by Chrome Web Store)
SCATERNET has a single purpose: as an entertainment novelty, while it is toggled
on it replaces the images, text, and sounds on web pages the user visits with
ska/scat-themed equivalents (cartoon images, scat-gibberish text, and ska/scat/
fart sounds) and overlays decorative stains.

## Permission justifications
- **storage** — remembers whether SCATERNET is on or off, plus the volume,
  mute, and "layer over videos" settings. Nothing else is stored; nothing is
  transmitted.
- **host access (`<all_urls>`)** — the whole point of the novelty is that it runs
  on every page you choose to visit while it is toggled on. It reads and rewrites
  the visible page content locally in your browser. It makes no network requests
  of its own — all images and audio are bundled with the extension.

## Data usage
SCATERNET does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. It makes no network
requests. All assets are bundled. State (on/off, volume) is stored locally via
chrome.storage.

## Notes for reviewers
- Broad host access is inherent to the product: a "make every page silly" novelty
  must run on every page. It has NO host_permissions and makes NO fetches — all
  content is bundled at the extension origin.
- Contains crude humour (fart sounds, brown "mud" stain overlays). No nudity or
  sexual content. Imagery is AI-generated photorealistic musicians (owned assets).

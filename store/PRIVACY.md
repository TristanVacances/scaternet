# SCATERNET — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-10_

SCATERNET is a joke browser extension that, while toggled on, rewrites the pages
you visit into a ska/scat parody (scat-gibberish text, cartoon images, ska/scat/
fart sounds, decorative stains).

## What data we collect
**None.** SCATERNET does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data,
browsing history, page content, or analytics.

## Network
SCATERNET makes **no network requests**. It has no `host_permissions` and no
remote servers. Every image and every sound is bundled inside the extension.

## Local storage
The extension stores a few settings **locally in your browser** using
`chrome.storage.local`: whether it is on or off, the volume, whether sound is
muted, and whether it layers audio over videos. This never leaves your device and
is removed when you uninstall the extension.

## Page content
While toggled on, the extension reads and rewrites the content of the pages you
visit **entirely locally, inside your browser**, to produce the parody. This
content is never recorded or sent anywhere.

## Permissions
- `storage` — to remember your on/off and audio settings.
- Access to page content on the sites you visit (`<all_urls>`) — required so the
  novelty can rewrite whatever page you have open while it is on.
